# Product

> Inferred from Keegan's written brief of 2026-10-15. Not yet confirmed in an interview.

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Homeowners and property owners in Cape Town** with a gutter need: new gutters or a replacement, a repair, or cleaning and maintenance. They want to understand what they need and start a conversation, often from a phone.
- **Pitch audience:** Cape Gutters' decision-makers, who see this as the proposed new website, presented by Keegan.

## Product Purpose

A marketing website and enquiry route for Cape Gutters, a gutter specialist in Cape Town. Keegan describes the client as a major South African gutter company.

Success: a visitor understands the three services and how a gutter system moves water from the roof to the drain, and sends an enquiry. The client sees an established, substantial specialist.

## Positioning

Specific, technical clarity about guttering, presented with architectural quality. Authority comes from clarity and detail, never from claims.

## Constraints

- Never publish "biggest", "leading", national coverage, credentials, guarantees, prices, phone numbers, addresses, qualifications, testimonials or statistics.
- Services (installation and replacement, repairs, cleaning and maintenance), materials and assessment arrangements are proposed until the client confirms them.
- Imagery is generated and illustrative until replaced with verified client photography. Never present it as completed work.
- The wordmark and symbol are a proposed identity until the client's existing brand assets are confirmed.
- Map regions are geographic references, not confirmed service coverage.
- The enquiry form is a demo until a delivery destination is configured.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, full keyboard and touch use, visible focus, and reduced-motion alternatives for every animation.

## Open decisions

- The client's existing logo, brand colours and photography.
- Confirmed service list, materials, coverage, contact details and enquiry destination.
